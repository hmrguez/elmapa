# Code of conduct

Elmapa welcomes people of every background and experience level. This code applies in issues, pull requests, reviews and other project spaces.

Discuss the change and its effect on mapmakers. Explain the reasoning behind review feedback, credit other people's work and give newcomers room to ask questions. Harassment, discrimination, threats, sexual advances, personal attacks and sharing someone's private information are unacceptable.

If you experience or witness a problem, contact a project maintainer privately. If you need a private contact method, open an issue asking for one and keep names, evidence and identifying details out of that request.

Maintainers may edit or remove harmful content, close a discussion, warn participants or restrict participation. They will consider context, severity and patterns of behavior, and limit disclosure of reports to the people needed to address them.
