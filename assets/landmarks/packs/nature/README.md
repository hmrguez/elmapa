# Nature Landmarks

Sixteen original sepia vignettes for coastlines, deserts, forests, glacial waters, and exposed rock. Stable specimen IDs have the prefix `elmapa.nature:`. The cenote and waterfall use high-contrast previews in the orbit.

- **Oasis and dunes.** Full crowns and hanging skirts distinguish the fan palms. A barchan dune bends forward into two horns, with a steep leeward face between them. [Joshua Tree fan palm oases](https://www.nps.gov/jotr/learn/nature/oases.htm), [Great Sand Dunes dune types](https://www.nps.gov/grsa/learn/nature/dune-types.htm)
- **Cave, canyon, and cenote.** A shallow, dark entrance sits under broken ledges. The canyon exposes strata above the river. Water and hanging roots sit inside the cenote's rim. [Mammoth Cave entrance](https://www.nps.gov/places/mammoth-cave-historic-entrance.htm), [Grand Canyon geology](https://www.nps.gov/grca/learn/nature/grca-geology.htm), [INAH cenote photograph, fig. 2](https://revistas.inah.gob.mx/index.php/glifos/article/download/13042/14166/26083)
- **Reef and sea arch.** Antler-shaped elkhorn coral marks the reef, and pale surf crosses the opening of the arch. [NOAA elkhorn coral](https://sanctuaries.noaa.gov/pgallery/pgflorida/living/living_7.html), [NPS coastal erosion diagrams](https://www.nps.gov/subjects/caves/sea-or-littoral-caves.htm)
- **Waterfall, geyser, and volcano.** A tall waterfall narrows against a cliff. The geyser's narrow jet rises above a mineral cone, and the volcano has a cupped summit. [Bridalveil Fall](https://www.nps.gov/places/000/bridalveil-fall.htm), [Yellowstone hydrothermal features](https://www.nps.gov/yell/learn/nature/hydrothermal-features.htm), [USGS Lassen Cinder Cone](https://www.usgs.gov/volcanoes/lassen-volcanic-center/science/cinder-cone)
- **Basalt columns, hoodoos, and glacier.** Hexagonal tops distinguish basalt columns. Irregular bands and capstones mark hoodoos. Angular crevasses cross the glacier's tongue. [USGS Devils Postpile](https://www.usgs.gov/volcanoes/long-valley-caldera/science/vertical-columns-volcanic-rock-devils-postpile-national), [Bryce Canyon hoodoos](https://www.nps.gov/brca/learn/nature/hoodoos.htm), [Glacier Bay glacier anatomy](https://www.nps.gov/glba/learn/nature/anatomy-of-a-glacier.htm)
- **Mangrove, saguaros, and petrified log.** The vignettes emphasize tidal prop roots, upright cactus arms, and mineralized rings exposed by a fracture. [NOAA mangroves](https://floridakeys.noaa.gov/plants/mangroves.html), [NPS saguaros](https://www.nps.gov/articles/000/sagu-saguaro-cacti-plant-story.htm), [Petrified Forest wood](https://www.nps.gov/pefo/learn/nature/petrified-wood.htm), [NPS petrified wood rings](https://www.nps.gov/prwi/learn/nature/petrifiedwood.htm)

The palms grow from their own roots before their fronds open. The waterfall unfurls from its lip before spray reaches the pool, and the hoodoos rise before their caps settle. The longest artwork assembly is 720 ms.
