# HeRo's Brilliant pack

The observatory, market, fountain, and obelisk share the Basic pack’s parchment faces, sepia contours, and muted shadows. Faceted planes, straight folds, and selectively etched seams give the landmarks their geometric character. The fountain borrows the Basic pack’s sage water accents, and the monument’s diamond inlay echoes the pack glyph.

## Visual references

- The meeting of a broad curve and sharp diagonals in Joost Schmidt’s lithograph informed the observatory dome and the monument’s split planes. [Bauhaus exhibition poster, Museum of Modern Art](https://www.moma.org/collection/works/6235)
- Otl Aicher’s Munich 1972 signage and posters informed how each silhouette stays readable from a handful of shapes. [The Rainbow Games, International Design Center Berlin](https://www.otlaicher.de/en/articles/the-rainbow-games/)
- The way separate paper shapes move into readable figures in Norman McLaren and Evelyn Lambart’s animation informed the staggered placement. The individual animation parts are drawn in the landmark SVGs. [Le merle, National Film Board of Canada](https://www.nfb.ca/film/le_merle/)

## Placement

Each landmark starts with a base that stamps into place. The observatory builds up to its dome before the star pivots into view. The market opens its striped awning and adds two bundles of produce. The fountain unfurls one water arc at a time and finishes with a central drop. The monument stacks its steps, raises the shaft, and stamps the diamond inlay.
